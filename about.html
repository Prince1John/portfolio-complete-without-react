The person behind the projects

Seven years of building, designing, filming and fixing — usually all in the same week.

bio.txt

Hi, I'm John.

I started out fixing office computers in Nairobi while teaching myself to code at night. Somewhere along the way I picked up graphic design to make my own websites look better, then video editing because a friend's wedding needed someone behind the camera. Seven years later, all four skills pay the bills.

I work mostly with small businesses, churches and independent creators — people who need something done properly but don't have (or want) an in-house team. My job is to be that team, one project at a time.

When I'm not at a keyboard, I'm usually mentoring younger developers in Nairobi's tech community or tinkering with a new home network setup.
